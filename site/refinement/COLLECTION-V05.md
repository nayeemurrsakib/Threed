## Remaining individual work

Review each object against the camera at comparable size, from more than one
angle and with a second palette. Resolve role-specific defects before calling it
curated. Current examples needing more art direction include the controller's
surface transitions, the blender motor deck, the backpack construction and
inappropriate detached details on the animal and fabric studies. Curation can
mean removing an object from a release rather than filling a count target.

Do not release or describe this entire collection as professionally curated
because generation, structural validation or round-trip checks pass. See
`docs/QUALITY-STANDARD.md` and `AGENTS.md` for the persistent working rule.
