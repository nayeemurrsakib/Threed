# Functional hover review / 0.6.1

Current user rule: every illustration stays assembled and makes a slight gesture
from real use. This supersedes the old camera explosion exception. Geometry and
material finish remain individually reviewable studies, not a bulk quality claim.

The twenty reported issues received individual revisions: connected tape feed;
clock without extra crystal; fixed hourglass with transferring sand; small key
wave; rounded radio front; hollow pen bore; connected headphone yokes; continuous
controller shell with surface-conforming sockets; fitted dark webcam front;
whole kettle pour; two toast slices; fixed pot handles; complete bottle loop;
fixed gift side ribbon and parcel front tape; whole sewn tote; feeding receipt;
warm lantern light; sewn cap with curved bill; small rocket lift and flame.
The parcel's inner flaps now stay flat; only its outer pair lifts slightly, avoiding
the crossing panels in the reported screenshot.

Remaining objects each have an explicit small action in source/hover_policy.py.
Unknown future IDs fail until an action is authored. The blender has a closed
rounded motor box, matching top, seated collar and continuously rotating blades.
The terminal has a fitted control deck and a card feeding through its front slot.
Thin speaker, display, console and microphone faces received independent planar
corner radii to fit their rounded housings.

The studio keeps Color Story across browsing and reloads. Six genuine material
palette renders cover the whole gallery (360 thumbnails). Individual material
edits carry globally by slot and use one temporary background renderer to refresh
custom gallery colors. The large intro was removed and the scene JSON control
became an outlined download button with an icon and clear label. Existing branding
is retained until the user supplies their assets.

Checks: all 60 assets have zero generic explosion offsets; attached assemblies
share transforms; the spring is invariant at 30/60/90/144 Hz; continuous blade
rotation integrates velocity and pauses without resetting its phase; page hinges
stay bound; exports preserve material alpha and functional metadata. CPU review
boards cover resting/active states and live WebGL checks cover the reported
objects, palette persistence, gallery recoloring and the revised controls.
