# threed® generation source

Tool: built-in `image_gen` (no CLI/API fallback).

The flat concept was generated from `references/lighter-concept.png`, then
reproduced as outlined SVG paths. The 3D icon was generated using the flat concept
as its shape reference. Its output retains genuine alpha transparency.

## Flat identity prompt

Use case: logo-brand. Redesign this reference as the next minimal identity for a 3D icon library now named EXACTLY 'threed®': six lowercase letters t h r e e d followed by a tiny superscript registered symbol. Do NOT write threedee. Retain the reference's soft rounded, slightly less-bold charcoal letterforms, warm ivory #F5F2EA background, vermilion #E2694B accent, and sophisticated very minimal layout. The NEW SIGNATURE CONCEPT: two lowercase e letters face one another, with the right e horizontally mirrored. Angle their lower curves toward each other so they form one beautiful compact HEART SILHOUETTE with a small central cleft, two round top lobes, and a taper toward a shared bottom point. It must visibly be made of two mirrored lowercase e forms, the e crossbars and counters remain readable inside the heart. The first e opens rightward, its mirrored partner opens leftward. Symmetrical, clever, affectionate, compact. No eyes, pupils, face, or mascot: the paired-letter heart replaces the old eye concept. In the wordmark threed®, subtly mirror the second middle e toward the first, keeping the name readable and the letters on the baseline. The standalone mark below should be the more expressive rotated two-e heart. Create a FLAT VECTOR-STYLE CONCEPT BOARD, landscape 1536x1024. Only three elements with enormous whitespace: a small quiet header at top left reading 'threed® / Identity'; a large single-line charcoal wordmark 'threed®' centered in the upper half; one beautifully resolved LARGE standalone solid charcoal two-e heart centered in the lower half. Put the wordmark within approximately x300..1240 y210..430 and standalone heart within x600..936 y600..910. Thin understated rule at y520 separates them. Do not add other small text, color swatches, taglines, tiles, slogans, extra icons, shadows or mockups. Black/charcoal flat letterforms on a perfectly uniform ivory background. The wordmark and paired-e-heart symbol should have smooth exact contours ready for vector reproduction. The heart mark is the main creative challenge: it must read both as an affectionate heart and a pair of inward-facing e letters.

## 3D icon prompt

Use case: stylized-concept. Create a single premium 3D brand icon based EXACTLY on the standalone mirrored-ee HEART SYMBOL in the lower half of the reference board. The reference board is only a geometry/style guide; output ONLY the isolated 3D icon, no wordmark, no registered symbol, no board, no labels, no square tile, no extra graphics. Preserve the heart silhouette and the clever two inward-facing lowercase e letters: rounded symmetrical top lobes, two open semicircular counters at the upper lobes, two horizontal inward-facing crossbars, the heart-shaped open negative space at the center, and the joined pointed base. The front face should faithfully match the reference symbol, not a generic heart. Turn that flat shape into a beautiful substantial sculpted 3D object in warm vermilion #E2694B, one uniform material: satin ceramic with softly rounded bevels, gently polished curved edges, restrained specular highlights. Not metallic, no chrome. Moderate extrusion depth roughly 18% of overall width, visible rounded sidewalls. Camera almost front-on, slight three-quarter view with a very small turn to reveal the right side and top, orthographic product photography. Preserve immediate legibility of both e letters and heart. Soft oversized studio light upper-left, subtle ambient occlusion inside the letter counters, realistic soft contact shadow if possible while keeping a genuinely transparent alpha background. Centered square composition, icon occupies about 72% of canvas with generous transparent padding, high resolution crisp production asset, beautifully minimal. No pupils or facial features.
